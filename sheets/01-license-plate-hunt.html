License Plate Hunt

Spot plates from as many states as you can before you park for the night.

How to play

  • Each player (or team) gets one copy of the state list below.
  • Spot a plate out the window, in the oncoming lanes, or in parking lots and gas stations.
  • Check the box once per state. First to fill a line of ten wins a snack pick; first to finish wins the trip.
  • Only real, readable plates count. Dealer and novelty plates don’t.

Van mode

Split the van into seat-teams: front seat + one back seat each. The middle aisle gets a bye. Small decorators can type the state name out loud — a grown-up marks the box.
