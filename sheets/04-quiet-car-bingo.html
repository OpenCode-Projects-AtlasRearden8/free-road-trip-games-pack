Quiet-Car Bingo

Find-it bingo for the low-energy hours. Point, mark, whisper. Nobody has to talk much.

How to play — the quietest game in the pack

  • Parallel play: everyone has their own board, nobody races out loud and nobody shouts BINGO.
  • See a thing, mark the box. A quiet point so others can spot it too is allowed and encouraged.
  • Mark five in a row and hold up your board. The grown-up in the front seat gives a thumbs up. That’s the whole celebration.
  • Good for the hour before naps, the hour after migraine warnings, and any leg driven by someone with just enough patience for a thumbs up.
