Story Relay

Everyone adds one line. Cards keep the story moving when imaginations stall.

How to play

  • Cut out the prompt cards below and keep them in an envelope in the glovebox.
  • Start a story: “One morning, a raccoon knocked on our minivan window...”
  • Going around the car, each person adds one sentence. Pass back the other way the next round.
  • When the story stalls, the staller draws a card and must fold it in somehow. This is the engine. It never fails.
  • A story ends when it returns to the person who started it, who adds a satisfying last line.

Silly-turn cards

Use these when a story gets too sensible. Draw one and it must become part of the very next sentence.
