Twenty Questions Cards

Classic yes-or-no guessing, with starter cards so the chooser is never stumped.

How to play

  • One person thinks of something, then silently draws a card to check the category is fair.
  • Everyone else asks yes-or-no questions: “Is it alive?” “Is it bigger than our van?”
  • The group gets twenty questions total, then one guess. If nobody gets it, the chooser wins.
  • Grandparents pick the hardest cards on purpose. Kids pick the silliest ones. Both strategies work.
  • The judge rule: one neutral adult breaks ties on whether an answer counted as yes, no, or “sort of.”

Starter cards — cut out, pick one, think of something that fits

ANIMALSomething with feathers, fur, or scales. Bonus points if it could survive our trunk. FOODSomething that could be served at a rest stop, for better or worse. PLACESomeplace on this trip’s route or a place we’ve visited together. THINGS ON WHEELSA vehicle of any kind. Every yes/no question it answers honestly.
IN OUR VANSomething actually sitting inside this vehicle right now. MOVIE or SHOWSomething every player has probably seen. Keep it fair. WEATHERA kind of weather or season. Alert the driver if guesses get loud. JOBSomething people do for work. Choose from a job anyone here might find interesting.
FAMILY MEMORYSomething from a trip or holiday we all remember. MADE-UPA character or creature from a story someone here invented. MYSTERY OBJECTSomething found in a grandparent’s kitchen drawer. HARD MODESomething that makes a sound. Guessers get twenty questions and one guess.

Question counters

Mark one box for each question asked. When all twenty are filled... one final guess, then the story of what it was.
