How to use this pack

  • Print on plain letter paper (A4 also works). No signup, no ads, no apps.
  • Slip sheets into sheet protectors and use dry-erase markers to play again and again.
  • The bingo and card sheets are cut-and-play; keep an envelope in the glovebox.
  • Grandparents make excellent bingo callers and story-relay starters.

Tips for real trips

Motion-sensitive riders: bingo boards that only need quick glances out the window are kinder than reading sheets. Let motion-sick kids be the caller instead.

Low-energy drivers: every game here can run with you as a one-word participant. The pack is built so passengers carry it.

Van crowds: photocopy one bingo sheet per seat and let people play quietly instead of shouting “BINGO!” — a whisper wins count too.

Night legs: the license-plate hunt gets hard; swap to Twenty Questions and the story relay, which need everyone’s eyes for nothing.

Mixed ages: pair a grandparent and a small kid on one board. Adults hear the rules; kids do the checking. Win together.

Screen-light: this pack is meant to stretch the time between videos. One bingo round beats ten minutes of whining — most trips.
