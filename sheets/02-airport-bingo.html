How to play

  • One card per player, or pair up — two sets of eyes per card is great for small kids.
  • Spot a thing, mark its box. Five in a row across, down, or diagonal wins the round.
  • The winner picks the walk route, the boarding snack, or the radio station for the ride from the airport.
